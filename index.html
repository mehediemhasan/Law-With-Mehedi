Academic work

Research that informs the conversation

Two theses on how law responds to climate risk and to people forced to move.

LL.M monograph · BUP · Nov 2024

Climate-Adaptive River Management in Bangladesh: A Study on the Challenges from the Legal Perspective

Examines whether the Bangladesh Water Act 2013 and National Water Policy 1999 address climate risk in the Ganges-Brahmaputra-Meghna basin, and proposes reforms using models from the Netherlands, New Zealand, the Philippines, the Mekong and Japan.

LL.B dissertation · East West University · 2023

Rights of Migrants and Refugees and State Obligation under International Law: An Analysis under the Notion of Place of Safety

Analyses the legal framework protecting migrants and refugees, how the “place of safety” notion arose from maritime rescue law, and the gaps that let states avoid obligations. Supervised by Dr. Nabaat Tasnima Mahbub.
